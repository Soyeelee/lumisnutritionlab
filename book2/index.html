WHAT TO WATCH FOR
Anything that might have caffeine — especially in the evening.
WHERE CAFFEINE HIDES
Chocolate and cocoa (dark has more), cola, some iced teas, coffee ice cream, coffee candies. Energy drinks have the most; doctors say kids skip them.
HOW TO OBSERVE
Watch the clock, not just the food. When did you eat it, get in bed, and really fall asleep?
HOW TO RECORD
Write what you had, when, and when you finally fell asleep. Not sure it had caffeine? Write it down with a big fat ?.

For the grown-up lab partner · the one that matters

Praise the question mark.

A child who writes “I don’t know” has produced an honest result — harder and rarer than a confident one. Two of Jiwoo’s five entries are question marks. Answer that with “Well, chocolate still keeps you up,” and everything the story taught comes apart in one sentence. Answer this instead: “Good — you wrote down what you actually knew. That’s the whole job.”
