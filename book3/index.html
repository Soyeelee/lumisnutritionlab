WHAT TO WATCH FOR
Salty snacks — chips, pretzels, popcorn, ramen, pizza. Anything that makes your tongue go whoa.
HOW TO OBSERVE
This one is about your body’s bell. Every time you eat something salty, count how many times you actually drink water. Notice your mouth and your head.
HOW TO RECORD
Write what you ate, how many times you drank water, and what you noticed. Not sure it was thirst? A question mark is a real answer.

For the grown-up lab partner · the one that matters

Praise the blank day.

A child who writes nothing has produced an honest result — harder and rarer than a full row. Three of Jiwoo’s seven days are blank, on purpose. Answer that with “Well, too much salt is still bad for you,” and everything the story taught comes apart in one sentence. Answer this instead: “Good — you left it empty because it was empty. That’s the whole job.”
