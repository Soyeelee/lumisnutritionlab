Lumi’s Nutrition Lab is a series of illustrated chapter books for ages 6–9 (Grades 1–3), story and pictures by Soyee Lee. In each book, nine-year-old Jiwoo eats or drinks something ordinary, and a tiny scientist named Lumi records what her body does next — no lectures, no good or bad foods. Three cases are out now; seven more are on the way.

Paperback: Baninoon (Seoul). Ebook: Kindle on Amazon.
