For the grown-up lab partner · the one that matters

Praise “I didn’t feel anything.”

A child who reports no change has produced an honest result — harder and rarer than the expected one. Three of Jiwoo’s six logged days show nothing, on purpose. Answer that with “Well, sugar is still bad for you,” and everything the story taught comes apart in one sentence. Answer this instead: “Good — you wrote down what actually happened. That’s the whole job.”
